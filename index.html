Offline First

Every module above works fully without a network connection — sync is the exception, not the foundation.

Smart Expense Capture

Scan a receipt, not a spreadsheet.

Point the camera at a receipt, or paste a payment message shared from another app. On-device text recognition pulls the merchant, amount, and date — you confirm the category, and it becomes an Expense.

Or turn on Smart Expense Detection (optional, Android): when Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM or a supported bank app notifies you of a completed payment, HomeBook shows a "New expense detected" notification and puts it in Needs review for you to check and add — nothing is added on its own.

Google ML Kit and simple on-device rules — not a cloud AI request. No image, message or notification text ever leaves your phone to parse it, and notification text is never saved.

Budget pacing

A budget that tells you before you're over.

HomeBook checks your spending against your budget and the pace you're on to finish the month — entirely with arithmetic over numbers already on your device. You hear about it once each at 70%, 80%, 90% and 100%, never more.

No model, no server. Quiet hours from 10 PM to 7 AM, a switch for every kind of alert, and several events arrive as one summary — not a feed to monitor.

Data handling

Local-first, and optional cloud sync.

If you never sign in, every record and attached file stays on your device — only anonymous crash reports and usage counts are sent, never your records. Sign in only when you want a cloud backup, and even then your household's records and files go to a private store only your account can open. No ads, no data sale, no third-party trackers.
