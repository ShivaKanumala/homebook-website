Smart Expense Capture

Scan a receipt, not a spreadsheet.

Point the camera at a receipt, or paste a payment message shared from another app. On-device text recognition pulls the merchant, amount, and date — you confirm the category, and it becomes an Expense.

Budget pacing

A budget that tells you before you're over.

Once a day, HomeBook checks your category spending against your budgets and the pace you're on to finish the month — entirely with arithmetic over numbers already on your device.

No model, no server. If something's worth flagging, you get exactly one notification — not a feed to monitor.

Data handling

Local-first, and optional cloud sync.

If you never sign in, HomeBook collects nothing — every record lives in an on-device database. Sign in only when you want sync across devices, and even then, only your household's own records go to a private, access-controlled store. No ads, no data sale, no third-party trackers.
