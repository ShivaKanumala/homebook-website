01Introduction

HomeBook is a household management app for tracking the things, documents, vehicles, expenses, and reminders that make up running a home. HomeBook is local-first: every record you add is stored on your device whether or not you ever create an account. This policy explains what happens if you choose to sign in and sync.

02Information we collect

If you use HomeBook without signing in, we collect nothing — all your data stays on your device. If you choose to sign in (email/password or Google), we store your email address, display name, and a unique account identifier, provided by Firebase Authentication. If you enable cloud sync, the household records you create (Things, Vehicles, Documents, Warranties, Maintenance, Reminders, Expenses, Income, Savings, Recurring Payments, Budgets, Transfers, Lending & Borrowing records, Planned Expenses, Family Member entries, and Family Occasions) are copied to a private cloud data store tied to your account, together with any photos and PDF files you attach to those records. If a crash occurs, diagnostic crash reports (device model, OS version, stack trace) are sent via Firebase Crashlytics — never your household data.

03How we use your information

Your account and household data are used solely to provide sign-in, cloud backup, and multi-device sync for your own household, and crash reports are used solely to fix bugs. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties beyond the infrastructure providers (Google Firebase) that host it.

04Who can see your data

Your household's cloud data is only accessible to accounts you explicitly add as family members, according to the role (owner, editor, viewer) you assign them. Nobody outside your household — including us — has a way to browse your data through the app.

05Data storage and security

Local data lives in an on-device SQLite database and is never transmitted anywhere unless you sign in and sync. Cloud data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Storage (for photos/document files), protected by security rules that restrict every read and write to authenticated members of the relevant household. Receipt scanning (Smart Expense Capture) runs entirely on-device via Google ML Kit — no photo or text is ever sent anywhere to parse it. The same applies to payment messages you share or paste into HomeBook: they're read on your phone only. Notifications (budget alerts, reminders, festival and occasion heads-ups) are worked out on your phone from your own records — no server decides or sends them.

06Data retention

Local data stays on your device until you delete a record yourself or uninstall the app. Cloud data persists for as long as your account exists and you remain part of a household. When you delete your account (see Delete Account), your account profile and household membership are removed immediately; a small amount of information may be retained where we are legally required to (for example, financial or security logs required for fraud prevention), but this does not apply to ordinary household records, which are deleted as described on that page.

07Third-party services we use

HomeBook uses the following Google Firebase services, only when you choose to sign in: Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In (account creation and login), Cloud Firestore (household record sync), Firebase Storage (photo/document file sync), Firebase Crashlytics (crash diagnostics), and Firebase Remote Config (app version/feature flags — no personal data). None of these are used unless you sign in, except Crashlytics, which may report an anonymous crash even for a signed-out session so we can fix bugs that affect everyone.

08Advertising

HomeBook shows no ads and includes no advertising SDK. We do not use an Advertising ID for tracking or personalization. Google Play's Data Safety form may still list Advertising ID as a technically-available identifier because Firebase Analytics' underlying library can access it on the device — HomeBook does not read or use it for any purpose.

09Your choices & rights

You can use HomeBook fully offline without ever creating an account. You can sign out at any time — your local data is unaffected. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data from your device. You can request a copy or deletion of your cloud account and data at any time — see Delete Account for exactly how.

10Children's privacy

HomeBook is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated effective date.

In short: nothing leaves your device unless you choose to sign in. No ads, no data sale, no tracking beyond what running your account requires.

12Contact us

Questions about this policy, or a request to access or delete your cloud account and data? Reach out at support@homebook.app.
