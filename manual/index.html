Updated for version 1.2 — anything added since 1.0 is marked NEW.

Home dashboard

home

Tab 1. A rolled-up view of your whole household — every section below only appears when it has something to show.

Header

  • Time-of-day greeting + "Your Home"
  • Bell → Notifications NEW (dot when something is unread)
  • Search icon → Search
  • "Needs attention" badge when things are overdue

Sections, top to bottom

  • Keep your HomeBook safe / sync status card NEW — suggests signing in when you have data only on this phone; after sign-in shows "Syncing…" or "Waiting to sync" with Retry, and disappears once everything is synced
  • Home banner — around a festival or family occasion it turns into a warm festive card NEW (see Family & Occasions)
  • Needs attention (overdue reminders)
  • Smart Capture pending review
  • Quick access (Things/Vehicles/Documents/Reminders counts)
  • Money overview + monthly spending + insights
  • Upcoming reminders · Recent purchases
  • Active recurring payments · Savings goals
  • Recently added things
Each section has a "See all" link to its full screen. This is where the Quick Add "+" lives. When a new version is on Google Play, HomeBook offers the update in-app NEW.

Upcoming Money

upcomingMoneyNEW

What's coming up, and how much money to keep aside for it.

On this screen

  • Toggle: Next 7 days / Next 30 days

  • Items grouped by day ("In 2 days · Mon, 28 Sep")

Attachments

NEW

Photos and PDFs on Things, Vehicles, Documents, Warranties, Services and Expenses — all handled the same way.

Adding a file

  • "Add file" → Take photo · Choose from photos · Choose file
  • JPG, PNG, WebP or PDF, up to 10 MB each
  • Photos are shrunk to a sensible size first; the same file can't be attached twice

Each file shows

  • Thumbnail, name, size
  • Status: Waiting to sync · Uploading % · Synced · Upload failed (with Retry)
  • ⋮ menu: Open · Remove
A file is saved on your phone first, so it works offline and appears instantly; when you're signed in it uploads to your household's private cloud storage. Removing a file removes it from your other devices too. On another device, files download only when you open them.

Notification Center

On this screen

  • Unread ones highlighted

Actions

  • Tap → the screen it's about
  • "Mark all as read" · swipe/Dismiss
Opened from the bell on Home.

What HomeBook can tell you about

Types

  • Budget alerts — once each at 70%, 80%, 90% and 100%
  • Upcoming payments · Reminders
  • Vehicle insurance/PUC · Warranties ending
  • Debt reminders
  • Festivals & family occasions — with "Set aside" and "Maybe later"
  • Spending insights — an occasional end-of-day note
  • Backup reminders

Your controls (Settings)

  • Switch each type on or off
  • Quiet hours — nothing between 10 PM and 7 AM
  • "Show amounts and names" — off keeps money notifications vague on the lock screen
"Set aside" only opens Add Savings — nothing is saved until you confirm. Several events due together arrive as one summary rather than a stack of notifications.

Family & Occasions

family

The people in your household, for your records (like who paid) — and the family days worth remembering. Adding a member doesn't give them access to your HomeBook.

Family members

On this screen

  • Member list — avatar initial, name, role
  • Members now sync, so "Paid by" is the same on every device NEW

Actions

  • Header "+" or tap a member → Member sheet (Name, Email, Role — a label only; it doesn't grant access)
  • Footer: "Adding someone doesn't give them access to your HomeBook." NEW
  • Delete button per row

Family occasions

NEW

On this screen

  • Birthdays, anniversaries, housewarmings and other yearly days, soonest first
  • Each shows its type, date, "In 5 days" / "Today", and years completed (e.g. "25 years")

Add / Edit occasion

  • Occasion: 🎂 Birthday · 💐 Anniversary · 🏡 Housewarming · 🎉 Other
  • Name (e.g. "Amma's birthday") · Date
  • Mention it: 1 / 3 / 7 / 14 days before
  • Save · Delete
Occasions repeat every year (a 29 February date falls on 28 February in other years). HomeBook mentions them ahead with an offer to set money aside, lists them in Upcoming Money, and marks the day on Home.

The festive touch on Home

NEW

When

  • A festival within the next week (Sankranti, Ugadi, Vinayaka Chavithi, Dussehra, Diwali, Holi, Eid, Christmas, Republic Day, Independence Day)
  • A family occasion within the next 3 days
  • The day itself: "Happy Diwali!" or "🎂 Amma's birthday is today"

What changes

  • Only Home's banner — a warm card with a greeting and illustration
  • Before the day: a "Set aside" link to Add Savings
  • Everything else in HomeBook stays exactly as usual

Backup & Restore

backup

A portable export of your records — independent of cloud sync.

On this screen

  • "Last backup" card (date + record/file count)
  • Illustration shown only if never backed up

Actions

  • "Back up now" → exports and shares a file
  • "Restore from backup" → pick file → confirm (shows home name, record count, export date) → restore
Restoring is additive/overwrite-matching — it never deletes existing records. Photos and files aren't included in the backup file; when you're signed in they're kept safe in your household's cloud storage instead. HomeBook gently reminds you to back up if you haven't in a while NEW.

App Lock

appLock

An on-device PIN gate — separate from and unrelated to your cloud sign-in.

App lock settings

On this screen

  • "App lock" switch
  • "Change PIN" row (visible once enabled)

Actions

  • Enable → set PIN, then confirm it (min 4 digits)
  • Disable / change → requires entering the current PIN first

Lock screen

On this screen

  • Lock illustration · PIN field

Actions

  • "Unlock" — inline error on a wrong PIN
Shown in place of the whole app on cold start/resume while locked. There is no recovery path — the PIN is a local salted hash only, not tied to your account.
