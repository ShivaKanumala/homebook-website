HomeBook User Manual

Every screen in HomeBook, what it shows, and how to get there. Organized in the order you'd actually meet each screen — start with how the app is laid out, then work through each module.

Updated for version 2.3. Version 2.0 redrew every screen with calm colours and hand-drawn doodles; 2.1 lets the Smart assistant search all your records; 2.2 adds What's today, lending records and voice entry; 2.3 reorganises Home, Money and Reminders. Anything new or changed in 2.0 – 2.3 is marked NEW.

How the app is organized

Five tabs anchor everything. The floating "+" opens Quick Add — the fastest way into any module without hunting through menus.

02

Home dashboard

homeNEW

Tab 1. A rolled-up view of your whole household — every section below only appears when it has something to show.

Header

  • Greeting with your first name, e.g. "Good morning, Priya"
  • Today's date · your home's name (rename it in Profile)
  • Spark → Smart assistant NEW
  • Bell → Notifications (dot when something is unread)
  • Search icon → Search
  • "N need attention" under the date — how many items Needs you and Needs review hold NEW

Sections, top to bottom NEW

  • Needs you — reminders due soon or overdue, plus the bills, services, money you owe and card bills due in the next 7 days, and insurance, PUC, warranty and document dates. A reminder you set for one of these on its day stands in for it, so it's listed and counted once
  • Needs review — Smart Capture items waiting for you, with how many came from payment notifications
  • This month — spent so far, what's left from what you've earned, bills still due, and a one-line budget
  • Keep your HomeBook safe — suggests signing in when your data is only on this phone, or shows "Waiting to sync" with Retry; hidden once everything is synced
  • Monthly spending — the last 7 days as bars (tap a bar for that day) or by category
  • Coming up — upcoming payments and pending reminders not already in Needs you
  • Your home — four counts: Things · Vehicles · Documents · Reminders, with your things and vehicles
  • Festival or family occasion card, when one is near (see Family & Occasions) — "+2 more" lists the others when several are near
  • Otherwise "What's today?" — today's date in India, a holiday, festival, awareness day, event from history or a fact, labelled with which it is (and the state, for a regional one), and "Explore more"
  • Recent transactions
  • Insights — the top three from the Smart assistant; "See all" opens it
  • Savings goals
Most sections have a "See all" link to their full screen. The "+" opens Quick Add. When a new version is on Google Play, HomeBook offers the update in-app.

Today in India

NEW

On this screen

  • The full date, e.g. "Tuesday, 10 November 2026"
  • Today's highlight
  • Holidays, festivals and days — each labelled National holiday, Festival, Awareness day or International day, with the state for a regional one
  • In history · Culture · Did you know?
  • Sources — tap one to open it in your browser

How it works

  • Dates follow India time (IST), whatever time zone your phone is in, and change at midnight
  • Built into the app — works offline, nothing is downloaded or sent
  • On a date with nothing listed, you see a fact about India instead
  • Festival dates follow the lunar calendar and can differ by a day between regions
Open it from "Explore more" on Home's What's today card.

Reminders

remindersNEW

Tab 4. Renewal dates, bills, service due dates — anything with a deadline, with a notification at the time you choose.

Reminders tab

NEW

On this screen

  • Tabs with counts: Today · Upcoming · Overdue · Completed
  • Reminders in one card, each with a tick circle and its category's doodle
  • Each row says when, e.g. "Today, 19:00 · Every month" — "Overdue" in red, "Snoozed" when snoozed
  • Everything else that's due in the next 30 days, alongside your reminders NEW: recurring payments not yet paid, services, card bills, money you owe, and insurance, PUC and document expiries (an expired one shows "Expired")
  • When a list is empty, "Add reminder" right there
  • Bell → Notifications

Actions

  • "+" → Add reminder
  • Tick → done (or not done), with Undo
  • Tap → the reminder's page; tap a bill, service, renewal or expiry → the record it belongs to NEW
  • Long press → Edit or Delete
Bills, services and expiries are shown from their own records and have no tick — pay or renew them where they live. If you set a reminder for the same thing on the same day, only your reminder is shown.

Reminder page

reminderDetailNEW

On this screen

  • Title, category, and a "Snoozed" chip when snoozed
  • When — date and time; "Originally" shows the date it had before a snooze
  • Repeat · Remind me · Notes
  • Linked to — the thing, vehicle or lent/borrowed record NEW, tap to open it

Actions

  • "Mark as done" / "Mark as not done" — on a reminder about money lent or borrowed, it asks whether to record the payment too, and you enter the amount NEW
  • "Snooze 1 day" — an overdue reminder moves to tomorrow, an upcoming one a day later
  • "Edit" · pencil and bin in the top bar
A snooze moves only when you're reminded: the reminder's own date and repeat stay as they were. Marking it done, or changing its date or time, ends the snooze.

Add / Edit reminder

addReminder

Fields

  • "What should we remind you about?" — the title
  • Category NEW: Bills · Insurance · Service · Vehicle · Documents · Health · Home · Family, or None
  • Date · Time (optional, can be removed) NEW
  • Repeat: Does not repeat · Daily · Weekly · Monthly · Yearly
  • Remind me: On the day · 1, 3 or 7 days before · 1 month before
  • Notes NEW
  • Linked thing (locked when opened from a thing or vehicle)

Actions

  • "Save reminder" / "Save changes"
Repeat is shown on the reminder, but HomeBook doesn't yet create the next one when you mark it done.

Reminder notifications

NEW

When

  • At the reminder's time — 9 AM if it has none — less what "Remind me" says
  • On its new day when snoozed with "Snooze 1 day"
  • Still on time after the phone restarts or HomeBook updates

On the notification

  • "Done" — marks it done, and the change syncs to your other phones
  • "Snooze 1 hour" — on this phone only
  • Tap → the reminder
Needs notification permission. HomeBook uses Android's ordinary (inexact) alarms, so a notification can arrive a few minutes late on some phones.

Money & Cards

money

The one screen that ties all your household money together for the month — or for your salary cycle. It's Tab 3 (also reachable from Profile → Money).

Money tab

NEW

On this screen

  • "Money" with the period under it; calendar button → Money Calendar, download button → PDF statement
  • Period switcher — a calendar month, or a salary cycle that starts on the day you choose (e.g. the 25th to the 24th)
  • One summary card NEW — Earned · Spent · Left and the net cash flow: money in minus money out, not your bank balance (income received, transfers (net), still due, income still to come, and what's left after it)
  • Balance now — only if you add an opening balance on this device
  • Month-end estimate — labelled as an estimate: what's in so far, plus income still expected, less what's still due; new spending isn't included
  • "Set up your money plan" — a short line under the summary, until you run Money Setup or tap "Not now"
  • Shortcuts: Smart Capture · Lending & borrowing · Upcoming money · Smart assistant
  • Spending, with a switch NEW: Bars (Daily, Weekly or Monthly; tap a bar for it, "See entries" for the list) · Calendar (each day shaded from less to more, with the daily average and the highest day) · Categories (a ring, each category in its own colour; "Explore" opens the Breakdown)
  • Bills and commitments — the bills reserve, once you have a plan
  • Budget — two rings, time passed and budget spent: "On pace", above an even pace, or over budget; without a budget, a line to set one
  • Smart insights — the top one, full width

More in Money

  • Track — Expenses · Income · Transfers · Spending analytics
  • Plan — Upcoming Money (next 7 days) · Recurring · Planned expenses · Savings
  • Cards and dues — Cards & Wallet · Money waiting for you · Money you owe
  • Views — Money Calendar · Money Timeline
A budget carries forward to the next period until you change it; set it from Expenses. A rent or EMI you record by hand is matched to its recurring payment, so it is counted once, not twice. The salary-cycle day is set in Settings → Money and shared with your household's other phones when you're signed in; the opening balance stays on this phone.

Cards & Wallet

cards

On this screen

  • Your cards as a swipeable stack of card faces — credit, debit and prepaid; Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Amex, Maestro or Other
  • Each face shows the bank, card name and "•••• 1234" — the last four digits only
  • Available credit across your credit cards, from the balances you've recorded

Actions

  • "Add card" · tap a card → its details
HomeBook isn't connected to your bank. It stores the last four digits only — never the full number, CVV, PIN or OTP.

Add / Edit card

Fields

  • Bank (optional shortcut) · Bank / issuer · Card type · Network
  • Card product · Nickname · Name on card · Last 4 digits · Expiry — all optional
  • A card style, with a live preview of the card face
  • Credit cards only: Credit limit, Outstanding balance (recorded by you), Statement day, Payment due day, Bill reminder, Annual fee, Rewards notes
  • Notes

Good to know

  • Switching a card to debit or prepaid hides the credit fields and drops them on save
  • Text that looks like a full card number is refused, with the field marked
  • Bill reminder: if the card's bill is already a recurring payment, pick it, so the bill shows once in Upcoming, not twice

Card detail

On this screen

  • The card face · Details
  • Credit: limit, manually recorded outstanding balance, available credit and how much of the limit is used
  • Statement & payment: next statement and payment due
  • Fees & rewards · Paid with this card — expenses linked to it

Actions

  • "Record payment" — lowers the recorded balance; it is not an expense, so nothing is counted twice
  • Edit · Delete
Purchases stay ordinary expenses: when an expense is paid by credit or debit card, Add Expense lets you pick which card. A credit card's payment due date appears in Upcoming Money.

Money Setup

moneySetup

Steps (all optional)

  • Your pay — take-home pay, how often (Monthly, Every 2 weeks, Weekly, Varies) and payday; monthly pay can be added to Income as one monthly salary from the next payday
  • Budget view — Calendar month or Salary cycle (needs a monthly payday), on this phone
  • Bills reserve — a target, when you move it (after payday, on a set day, or not planned) and account names
  • Regular commitments — tick the recurring payments the reserve covers, or add rent, EMIs, insurance, utilities and bills
  • Opening balance — optional, on this phone; not your salary and not a budget
  • Review → Save

Good to know

  • "Skip" leaves at any step; nothing is saved
  • A commitment already in Recurring is linked, not copied; new ones start at their next due date
  • Account names only — anything that looks like an account number is refused
  • Run it again any time from Settings → Money → Money setup to change your answers
Offered after onboarding, as a card on Money, and in Settings. Payday, the reserve plan and covered commitments are shared with your household's other phones when you're signed in.

Bills reserve

On this card

  • Reserve target · Planned transfer and its date
  • Bills covered this period · Paid so far · Still due
  • A note when the bills are more than the target
  • Set aside (as you recorded) · Left in reserve (estimate)
  • Your account names, e.g. "Salary account → Bills account"

Actions

  • "I've set money aside" — note how much you moved this period
  • "Edit plan" → Money Setup
A plan, not a bank balance. Nothing here is counted as spending — bills count once, when they're paid — and HomeBook never moves money.

Smart Expense Capture

smartCapture

Turn a receipt, payment message or (optionally) a payment notification into an Expense without typing it in — parsed entirely on-device, and always reviewed before it's added.

Capture entry

On this screen

  • "Say it" and "Type a sentence" NEW, then Scan Receipt · Paste Transaction · Share to HomeBook · Add Manually
  • "Detect payment notifications" row with its status (Android)
  • "Needs review" list of pending candidates

Actions

  • Say it → your phone's speech screen: say "Spent 800 at a restaurant using UPI" NEW
  • Type a sentence → one line, e.g. "Paid 1,200 for groceries yesterday" NEW
  • Both open Review capture with the amount, category, date and payment method filled in from what you said — in English, Hindi or Telugu
  • Scan Receipt → camera/gallery → OCR parse
  • Paste Transaction → text dialog
  • Share to HomeBook → instructions (actually triggered from another app's share sheet)
  • Add Manually → Add Expense
  • Detect payment notifications → Smart Expense Detection

Smart Expense Detection

smartDetection

On this screen

  • Switch with status: Off · On — watching for payment notifications · "Notification access is currently disabled." with Enable
  • How it works
  • Privacy — notification text processed in memory on the phone, never saved or uploaded; only amount, merchant, date, payment method, category and a hashed reference kept until you review
  • Turn off (when on)

Actions

  • Switch on → HomeBook's explanation sheet → "Enable Notification Access" opens Android's Notification access page for "HomeBook payment detection"; "Not now" changes nothing
  • Enable → Android's page again (only when you tap it)
  • Turn off → stops immediately; a snackbar offers Android Settings to also remove the access
Off by default; Android only. Reached from Settings or the Smart Capture screen. Only notifications from supported payment and bank apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Amazon, CRED, MobiKwik and major banks) are looked at; chat notifications and SMS are never read. Detected payments appear under "Needs review", and as a "New expense detected" notification with Review & Add and Ignore — also while HomeBook is closed. It turns off when you sign out or another account signs in.

Review capture

smartCaptureReview

On this screen

  • Confidence label — or, for a payment notification, what it looks like: Expense / Income / Refund / Transfer detected, or "Review transaction"
  • "Source: payment notification" line, and a hint for transfers and refunds
  • Duplicate-detection banner if flagged — including the same bill scanned twice (same bill number and amount) NEW
  • Editable pre-filled fields: Amount, Merchant, Category, Subcategory, Date, Payment method, Notes
  • Payment method read from the message — UPI, credit or debit card, NEFT/IMPS/RTGS as bank transfer; UPI is filled in for payments from UPI apps
  • Merchant is optional: leave it blank and the expense is saved under its category name; a merchant you add is remembered for next time
  • Merchant memory: a merchant you've categorised before comes pre-filled with the same category and subcategory
  • "Suggested category — please check" when the category guess is uncertain
  • Receipt photo — tap to see it full screen and zoom NEW
  • "Receipt details" fold-out: bill number, items, subtotal, taxes (CGST, SGST, GST…) and discount, with "Copy items into notes" NEW
  • Raw source text shown in a muted box (pasted/shared/scanned only — notification text is never kept)
  • Money received from a notification is reviewed as Income: Amount, Source, Date, Notes

Actions

  • "Ignore"
  • "Confirm & Add" (or "Add anyway" if a duplicate was flagged) · "Add income" for money received
  • Duplicate banner: "View existing" / "Use existing"
