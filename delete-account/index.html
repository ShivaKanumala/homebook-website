Account & Data · HomeBook

Delete Your Account

Effective 22 September 2026 · support@homebook.app

You can delete your HomeBook sign-in and its cloud footprint two ways: right in the app, or by emailing us if you no longer have it installed. Either way, your on-device data is a separate thing — see below for exactly what goes and what stays.

In the app

Delete from Settings

  1. Open HomeBook and sign in
  2. Go to Settings → Account
  3. Tap Delete account
  4. Confirm Delete Account in the dialog

Takes effect immediately. If you signed in a while ago, HomeBook may ask you to sign in again first — that's Firebase requiring a recent login before a destructive action, not an extra step we added.

Without the app

Request by email

Uninstalled the app, or signed in on a device you no longer have? Email us from the address on the account and we'll delete it for you. This is a manual request we action ourselves — not an automated web form.

Email support@homebook.app →

Typically actioned within 7 days.

What actually gets deleted

Deleting your account removes your cloud footprint — the parts of HomeBook that exist because you signed in. It does not touch the household data stored on your own device, and it does not touch data other people in your household are relying on.

Deleted
  • Your Firebase sign-in (email/password or Google)
  • Your account profile — email, display name, account ID
  • Your membership in this device's household, on the server
Kept
  • Every Thing, Vehicle, Document, Expense, Reminder etc. on this device
  • The shared household's cloud data — other members keep their access
  • Receipts/documents already attached to the household's records

If legally required to retain something

We don't keep financial account data beyond what's described above. In the rare case a specific record must be retained to comply with a legal obligation (for example, an active fraud or security investigation), only that narrow record is kept, and everything else in "Deleted" above still goes.

If you also want your device wiped

Account deletion is separate from your local data. HomeBook works fully offline by design, so uninstalling the app is what removes everything stored on your device — there is nothing to separately "clear" beyond that. Deleting your account first, then uninstalling, covers both.
